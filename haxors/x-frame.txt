Apache2: 
Header always append X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN

specifically:
cd /etc/apache2/
nano conf-enabled/security.conf
at the top of the page, enter:
LoadModule headers_module /usr/lib/apache2/modules/mod_headers.so

then, uncomment the two "Header set..." values at the bottom of the page
Save the doc, then restart apache: /etc/init.d/apache2 restart



nginx:
add_header X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN;

wildfly:
(in resposne headers)
X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN
